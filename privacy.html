Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

FormFix is built with privacy first. All photo resizing, signature compression, 4×6 print sheet generation, and PDF conversion happen locally inside your own browser.

1. Zero File Upload

We do not upload, store, view, or save your personal photos, signatures, or PDF documents on any external server.

2. Local Preferences

Only your Light/Dark theme preference is saved locally on your device using browser localStorage.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Links

  • We may display third-party ads to keep FormFix free for everyone.
  • External links to partner utilities (IFSCPin and ReelSave) are governed by their own privacy policies.
