PRIVACY POLICY

Your Documents Never Leave Your Device

Last updated: November 2026

1. 100% Client-Side Processing (Zero File Upload)

FormFix processes all photos, signatures, Name/Date slates, 4×6 print sheets, and PDFs directly inside your web browser using local device memory. We do not upload, store, view, or transmit your personal photos or documents to any external server.

2. Information We Collect

FormFix does not require account registration or login. Your theme preference (Light/Dark mode) may be saved locally in your browser's localStorage purely for your convenience.

3. Analytics & Third-Party Advertising

We use privacy-friendly web analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand overall site traffic. We also display third-party advertisements (such as Adsterra) to keep FormFix completely free. Third-party ad partners may use standard cookies or non-personal browser signals to serve relevant ads.

4. Support Contact

If you choose to contact us via our Help Desk (WhatsApp or Email), we only use your message to reply to your support request.
