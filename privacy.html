Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 28, 2026

At FormFix (https://formfix-in.pages.dev/), your privacy is our top priority. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. 100% On-Device Processing (No Uploads)

All image resizing, cropping, signature preparation, and PDF conversion happen locally inside your browser. We do not have a server that receives your photos, signatures, or documents. Your files never leave your device.

2. Ads and Third-Party Cookies

FormFix is a free tool supported by advertisements. We use third-party ad networks (such as Google AdSense and other partners) to display ads. These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to collect non-personal data (like your browser type or general location) to serve relevant ads. They do not have access to the images or documents you process on our tool.

3. Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics (like Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity) to understand how users interact with our site. This data is anonymized and does not include the images you process.

4. No Account Required

You do not need to create an account to use FormFix. We do not collect your name, email, or phone number unless you voluntarily contact us via our Help Desk.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us via the Help Desk on our website.
