Signature 20KB Mein Kaise Compress Karein?

Published on November 28, 2026 • 3 min read

Indian government exam forms (jaise SSC, UPSC, Railway) mein signature upload karna hota hai. Signature ka size 10KB se 20KB ke beech hona chahiye. Agar aapka signature isse bada hai, toh form reject ho sakta hai. Is article mein hum aapko batayenge ki ise kaise compress karein.

Signature Requirements (Common Exams)

  • Dimensions: 140 x 60 pixels (SSC) ya 300 x 120 pixels
  • File Size: 10 KB se 20 KB
  • Format: JPG ya JPEG
  • Background: Plain white
  • Signature Type: Black ya blue ink, running hand

Signature 20KB Kaise Banayein?

FormFix free hai aur aapka signature server par upload nahi hota.

  1. FormFix par jayein.
  2. "Signature" mode select karein.
  3. Apna signature photo upload karein (white paper par sign karke photo khinchein).
  4. Target size mein 20 daalein.
  5. Width mein 300 aur Height mein 120 daalein.
  6. "Process Image" par click karein.
  7. Download karein.

Common Mistakes (Inse Bachein)

  • Signature blurry hona: Achhi quality mein sign karein.
  • Dark background: Hamesha white paper use karein.
  • Signature bahut chhota: Signature clear aur readable hona chahiye.
