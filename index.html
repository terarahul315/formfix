FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions Answered

Are my photos or documents uploaded to any server?

No. All resizing, cropping, Name/Date slate stamping, and PDF conversion happen 100% locally inside your browser. Nothing is stored or sent online.

How do I resize a photo to exactly 20KB or 50KB?

Upload your photo, tap the 20 KB or 50 KB Quick chip (or type any KB), and click Process Image. FormFix automatically adjusts compression to stay under your target KB.

How can I add Name and Date of Photo (Agniveer / SSC)?

In Photo mode, enter your Candidate Name and DOP (DD-MM-YYYY) in the optional slate boxes before clicking Process Image.

What is the 4×6 Print Sheet tool?

It arranges 6 copies of your passport photo cleanly on a 300 DPI 4×6 inch sheet (1800×1200 px) ready for home or studio printing.

Is FormFix completely free to use?

Yes, all core photo, signature, print sheet, and PDF tools are free with no account or sign-up required.
