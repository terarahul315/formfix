PAN Card Photo 18KB Kaise Banayein: Step-by-Step

Published on November 28, 2026 • 3 min read

NSDL (jo PAN card issue karta hai) ki website par photo upload karte waqt ek strict rule hai: photo ka size 18KB se zyada nahi hona chahiye. Bahut log yahan atak jate hain. Is article mein hum aapko step-by-step batayenge ki ye kaise karein.

PAN Card Photo Requirements (NSDL)

  • Dimensions: 132 x 170 pixels (Width x Height)
  • File Size: Maximum 18 KB
  • Format: JPG ya JPEG
  • Background: Plain white
  • Photo Type: Recent passport style

18KB Photo Kaise Banayein? (FormFix ke saath)

FormFix free hai aur aapka photo kisi server par upload nahi hota. Bas ye steps follow karein:

  1. FormFix homepage par jayein.
  2. Apna photo upload karein.
  3. "Photo" mode select karein.
  4. Target file size mein 18 daalein.
  5. Width mein 132 aur Height mein 170 daalein.
  6. "Process Image" par click karein.
  7. Result aane par download kar lein.

NSDL Portal Par Upload Karne se Pehle

  • Photo ka background white hona chahiye.
  • Chehra clear dikhna chahiye.
  • Photo recent ho (6 mahine se purana nahi).
  • Photo blurry na ho.

Conclusion

PAN card ke liye 18KB photo banana ab mushkil nahi hai. FormFix ke saath, aap 30 second mein exact size ka photo bana sakte hain — bina kisi studio ke, bina kisi paise ke.
