SSC Photo Size 2026: Exact Requirements (20-50KB)

Published on November 28, 2026 • 4 min read

Har saal lakhon students SSC (Staff Selection Commission) exams ke liye apply karte hain. Lekin ek chhoti si galti — galat size ka photo upload karna — form reject ho jata hai. Is article mein hum aapko SSC CGL, CHSL, aur MTS ke liye exact photo requirements batayenge.

SSC Photo Requirements (2026)

SSC ne apni official website par photo ke liye ye specifications di hain:

  • Dimensions: 413 x 531 pixels (Width x Height)
  • File Size: 20 KB se 50 KB ke beech
  • Format: JPG ya JPEG
  • Background: White ya light colored (plain)
  • Photo Type: Recent (6 mahine se purana nahi), passport style

Signature Requirements

Signature ke liye alag specifications hain:

  • Dimensions: 140 x 60 pixels
  • File Size: 10 KB se 20 KB
  • Format: JPG ya JPEG
