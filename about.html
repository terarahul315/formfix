About FormFix

FormFix was born out of a simple frustration: why is it so hard to resize a photo for an Indian government exam form?

We saw students running to cyber cafes, paying money just to shrink a 2MB selfie down to 50KB. We saw privacy concerns — uploading personal photos to random servers. We decided to fix this.

FormFix is a 100% on-device tool. That means your photos, signatures, and documents are processed entirely inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Your privacy is not a feature; it's our foundation.

Our Mission

To make form-filling effortless for every Indian student, applicant, and CSC center — free of cost, free of clutter, and free of privacy risks.

Our Tools

We offer Photo Resizing (with optional Agniveer/SSC Name & Date slate), Signature Resizing, 4x6 Print Sheet generation, Image to PDF conversion, and format conversion (JPG/PNG/WebP).

Our Other Apps

We also build free daily utilities: IFSCPin (Bank IFSC & Pincode finder) and ReelSave (Instagram Reels & MP3 downloader).

If you have any feedback or need help, reach out to us via the Help Desk on the homepage.
